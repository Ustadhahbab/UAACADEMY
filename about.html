About

Scholar, counsellor and coach. One integrated approach.

UA Academy is led by Ustadh Ahbabur Rahman, who brings together Islamic scholarship, qualified counselling and practical business experience.

Ustadh Ahbabur Rahman

For more than 16 years Ustadh Ahbab has served as an Imam and Khateeb, guiding communities, developing young people and strengthening spiritual foundations. He has established two madrasahs serving over 400 students, creating structured environments where knowledge, discipline and character grow together.

Alongside his religious leadership he has founded and built four businesses. That real-world experience means he can mentor people not only in spiritual growth but also in performance, leadership and execution.

He works with clients who want more than inspiration: they want structure, accountability and measurable growth. His approach is practical and rooted in both sacred knowledge and lived experience.

He believes true success is not a choice between dunya and akhirah, but excelling in both through discipline, intention and action.
