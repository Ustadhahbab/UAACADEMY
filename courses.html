The 6 C’s of a Healthy Marriage

A 6-week online programme for Muslim couples.

Allah describes marriage as a source of tranquillity, with affection and mercy placed between spouses (Ar-Rūm 30:21). Most of us want that. Few of us are taught how to build it.

Many couples enter marriage with high hopes and little preparation. Then the first disagreements, unmet expectations and family pressures arrive, and they find they never learned the skills to handle them together.

This programme gives you those skills before small issues become big ones. Over six weekly sessions, you’ll work through the six foundations of a healthy marriage, rooted in Islamic teaching and practical coaching tools you can use straight away.

Who it’s for
  • Those preparing for marriage who want to start on solid ground
  • Newlyweds in their first years together, building the habits that will shape their marriage
  • Couples who want to strengthen their marriage relationship
What you’ll get
  • Six live, interactive online sessions (90 minutes each)
  • A free PDF copy of The 6 C’s of a Healthy Marriage on registration
  • The 6 C’s Marriage Assessment, which scores your marriage (or your readiness) on each C at the start and again at the end, so you can see your progress
  • A practical exercise each week to put into practice at home
  • Recordings of every session, so you never fall behind
  • A small group of like-minded Muslim couples, so you know you’re not alone
The six sessions

Six foundations, one week at a time.

01

Communication

Speak so you’re understood, listen so they feel heard

Why couples talk past each other, how to raise difficult topics without conflict, and the listening habit that changes everything.

02

Compassion

Seeing your spouse with mercy

Moving from criticism to understanding, responding to your spouse’s needs, and practising the rahmah Allah placed between you.

03

Compromise

Meeting in the middle without losing yourself

Handling differences in expectations, habits and family culture, and knowing what to hold firm on and what to let go.

04

Collaboration

From “you and me” to “us”

Working as a team on money, household, family and shared goals, and building a shared vision for your marriage.

05

Commitment

Choosing each other every day

What commitment means beyond the nikah, protecting your marriage from outside pressures, and staying connected when life gets busy.

06

Consistency

Making it last

Turning what you’ve learned into lasting habits, retaking the assessment, and creating your personal marriage plan for the year ahead.

FAQs

Common questions.

When does the programme start, and how much will it cost?

The start date and price will be announced soon. The programme will be very affordable so that every couple can take part. Register your interest and we’ll share the details with you first.

Do we both need to attend?

It’s recommended, and couples can join together on one booking. Individuals are also welcome.

I’m not married yet. Is this for me?

Yes. It’s designed for those preparing for marriage as well as newlyweds.

Will I have to share personal details in the group?

No. You choose what you share, and much of the reflection is private or done with your spouse.

Is this couples therapy?

No. It’s an educational coaching programme. If you’re experiencing serious difficulties, including any form of abuse, please get in touch privately and we’ll point you towards the right support.

What if I miss a session?

Every session is recorded, and the recording is shared only with registered participants, so you can catch up in your own time.
