Getting started

How do I book a session?

Book a free 20-minute consultation call on the contact page. We will talk through your goals, answer your questions and find a time that suits you.

Who do you work with?

Adults, young adults and teenagers, as well as children aged 8 to 16 through our children's coaching. Adult coaching and counselling is suitable for any age.

Are your services faith-based?

Yes. Islamic principles are woven into both coaching and counselling, which makes our services ideal if you want guidance that aligns with your values. Sessions are inclusive and supportive for people from all backgrounds.

What are your qualifications and experience?

Ustadh Ahbabur Rahman has taught, mentored, coached and counselled since 2007, and has served as an Imam and Khateeb. He holds an MA in Arabic and Islamic Theology and is a qualified counsellor, SFBT specialist and certified life coach.

Coaching & counselling

What is the difference between life coaching and counselling?

Life coaching is future-focused: it helps you set and achieve personal or professional goals. Counselling gives you space to explore and work through emotional or mental health difficulties. Both are client-centred; the focus and methods differ.

How is adult life coaching different from traditional counselling?

Traditional counselling may explore past issues in depth. Coaching at UA Academy is forward-focused, giving you strategies and insights to move towards your goals.

What issues can counselling and therapy help with?

Anxiety, stress, relationship challenges, low self-confidence and more, in a safe, non-judgemental environment where you can explore your feelings and work on solutions.

How does Solution-Focused Brief Therapy (SFBT) work?

SFBT is a short-term, goal-oriented approach that builds solutions rather than dwelling on problems. We use it to identify your strengths, clarify goals and develop practical next steps quickly.

How many sessions will I need?

The life coaching programme runs for 15 sessions. For counselling, the number varies with your goals and progress, and we review it with you as we go.

Children & families

What happens in a children's coaching session?

Sessions are interactive, positive and goal-focused, building confidence, resilience and life skills through activities and discussion, guided by SFBT techniques and an Islamic framework.

How do I know if coaching is right for my child?

If your child is struggling with confidence, motivation, behaviour or simply needs positive guidance, coaching can help. Commitment and willingness from your child are essential. Book a free call to talk it through.

Can parents be involved?

Yes, and we encourage it, especially for younger clients. Updates and tips for parents are available on request so good habits are reinforced at home.

Fees & practicalities

What are your fees?

Counselling is charged per session. The coaching programme is priced as a whole, with monthly payment plans available. Full details are on the Programmes & Investment page.

How long is each session?

Most sessions last 60 minutes. Couples counselling sessions are 90 minutes.

Do you offer in-person sessions?

All sessions currently take place online via Zoom to fit around your schedule.

Do you offer group sessions?

Yes, for some programmes, including children's workshops and group coaching for adults. Webinars are coming soon.
