The method

Your coaching journey, step by step.

Every client moves through seven stages. The pace and focus are tailored to you.

  1. 1

    Self-reflection

    Explore your current beliefs, values and goals.

  2. 2

    Goal setting

    Short and long-term aims using the SMART method.

  3. 3

    Skill building

    Strategies for confidence, resilience and focus.

  4. 4

    Mindset shift

    Face obstacles, learn from setbacks, cultivate shukr.

  5. 5

    Spiritual connection

    Faith in decisions; balancing worldly and spiritual aims.

  6. 6

    Action & accountability

    A personal action plan with weekly check-ins.

  7. 7

    Celebrate progress

    Recognise milestones and plan what comes next.
