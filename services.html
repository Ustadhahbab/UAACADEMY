Life Coaching

For adults and young adults seeking personal growth. Life coaching at UA Academy combines practical strategy with spiritual insight. Together we build a clear roadmap tailored to your goals, then work through it session by session with tasks and accountability in between.

  • Clarify your purpose and align your actions with your values
  • Overcome self-doubt, procrastination and limiting beliefs
  • Build routines that hold through busy and difficult seasons
  • Stay motivated with weekly accountability

Counselling & Therapy

A safe, confidential and non-judgemental space to explore your emotions, understand your challenges and work toward practical solutions. Sessions use Solution-Focused Brief Therapy and are tailored to you, with your faith respected throughout.

  • Anxiety, stress and low mood
  • Relationship and family difficulties
  • Self-confidence and self-worth
  • Working through past or present experiences
